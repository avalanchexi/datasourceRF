ICE
Gold Price
The LBMA Gold Price is set twice daily in auctions independently operated and administered by ICE Benchmark Administration Limited (IBA) and commencing at 10:30 and 15:00 London time. Sterling and Euro prices are available but they are indicative prices for settlement only.
Silver Price
The LBMA Silver Price is set daily in an auction independently operated and administered by ICE Benchmark Administration Limited (IBA) and commencing at 12:00 noon London time. Sterling and Euro prices are available but they are indicative prices for settlement only.
Platinum Price
The LBMA Platinum Price is set twice daily in auctions independently operated and administered by ICE Benchmark Administration Limited (IBA) and commencing at 9:45 and 14:00 London time. Sterling and Euro prices are available but they are indicative prices for settlement only.
Palladium Price
The LBMA Palladium Price is set twice daily in auctions independently operated and administered by ICE Benchmark Administration Limited (IBA) and commencing upon the conclusion of the LBMA Platinum Price auctions. Sterling and Euro prices are available but they are indicative prices for settlement only.
LBMA Precious Metal Prices are not published on either weekends or those days designated as UK bank holidays.
For specific future schedules, see ICE-IBA's website.

The LBMA Gold, Silver, Platinum and Palladium Price benchmarks are the global benchmark prices for unallocated gold, silver, platinum and palladium delivered in London, and are administered by
ICE Benchmark Administration Limited (IBA)
. A licence from IBA is required in order to obtain, use or redistribute real-time or historical benchmark data from that date, including for pricing and valuation activities and in transactions and financial products. Please contact
[email protected]
for details on how to obtain the requisite licence to receive and use the data.
